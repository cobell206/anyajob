How scoring works — and what your priorities add to the prompt.

Every role gets two 0–10 scores, combined into the overall score you sort by:

  • Qualification fit — how well your background matches what the role asks for.
  • Law-school value — how much the role strengthens your application to your target schools (legal exposure, brand-name employers, writing/research, pipeline programs).

The weighting below decides how those two combine into the overall number. Your résumé, keyword filters, and past thumbs up/down also feed in automatically — the 0–10 scale and what a good score means stay fixed, so scores stay comparable across roles.
